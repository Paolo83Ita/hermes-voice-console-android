VERDICT — Obsidian Night becomes the default identity (decision D-009): it wins every always-on criterion (power, burn-in, heat, ambience) that matters for a permanently displayed AMOLED console. Marble Light remains the daylight / inspection theme and the identity is preserved across both because the palette architecture, motifs, motion and state language are identical. An automatic time-based switch is proposed for V0.2 (Owner-configurable, never forced).
